FAQ

Questions, answered

What does the turnkey package include?

All 11 setup workstreams: company formation, regulatory licensing, banking solutions, payment infrastructure, CRM & back office, risk management, trading platform, prediction market platform, prop firm platform, digital banking and EMI infrastructure — coordinated by Bright Gate as one project.

How much does it cost?

The turnkey package starts from USD 51,600 and includes all of these services. For details on your setup, contact us and book a meeting.

Does Bright Gate issue the licence or run the brokerage?

No. Bright Gate provides advisory, development planning and execution coordination. Licences, bank accounts, EMI services and third-party technology remain subject to applicable requirements and independent provider approval. Company incorporation does not by itself grant a brokerage licence.

Which jurisdictions can be considered?

Depending on eligibility and intended activities, jurisdictions may include Mauritius, Seychelles, Vanuatu, Saint Lucia, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, Belize, British Virgin Islands, the Bahamas, Cayman Islands, Labuan. Every application remains subject to the decision of the relevant authority.

Which trading platforms can be used?

The architecture may incorporate MetaTrader 5, cTrader, Match-Trader and other professional platforms, subject to vendor eligibility and approval.

Can you support A-Book, B-Book or hybrid models?

Yes. Each brokerage is structured around its target markets, client profile, execution model and available capital, and the risk-management framework supports A-Book, B-Book and hybrid operations.
